# muna Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 November 2026
**App:** muna — money feels like home (iPhone)
**Developer:** Minaya Yagubova
**Contact:** [minaya.yagubova@icloud.com](mailto:minaya.yagubova@icloud.com)

muna is a personal budget app. It is built so that your financial
information stays on your iPhone and is never sent to the developer or
anyone else.

## The short version

- muna has **no account**, **no sign-in** and **no server**.
- Everything you enter stays **on your device**.
- muna contains **no analytics, no advertising and no tracking**, and no
  third-party code that does any of these.
- The developer only receives information if **you choose to send feedback
  by email**.

## What muna stores, and where

Everything below is stored only in muna's private storage on your iPhone.
Other apps cannot read it.

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Entries: amount, currency, category, date, note, repeat rule | To show your budget, totals and charts |
| Savings goals, loans, payment reminders, custom categories | The features you use them for |
| Settings: main currency, start day of the month, home page style, whether amounts are hidden | To remember your choices |
| Bill photos and documents you attach to entries | So you can open them later from the entry |

Your budget file and your attachments are protected with iOS data
protection: they cannot be read while your iPhone is locked.

muna does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, health data
or advertising identifier.

### Photos and files

When you attach a bill, muna uses the standard iOS photo picker or Files
picker. muna only receives the photo or file **you pick**. It does not get
access to the rest of your photo library or files, which is why iOS never
asks you for photo-library permission.

### Notifications

Payment reminders are scheduled by iOS on your device. They are not sent
through any server. You can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.

### Home Screen widget

The Quick add widget only opens muna's New Entry screen. It does not read
or display any of your data.

## Information that leaves your device, only when you choose

- **Backups.** In Settings → Backup you can export a backup file. You decide
  where to save or share it (for example Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or
  email). The backup contains your entries and settings, but not your
  attached bill files. Keep it somewhere safe.
- **Importing.** When you import a backup or a CSV file, muna reads only the
  file you pick.
- **Sharing a bill.** If you share an attached bill, it goes only to the app
  or person you choose in the iOS share sheet.
- **Feedback.** If you shake your iPhone or tap Settings → Send feedback,
  your mail app opens a draft to the developer. It includes the app version,
  the iOS version and the iPhone model. A shake also attaches a screenshot of
  the screen you were on, **which may show amounts**. You can read, edit or
  remove anything before sending, and nothing is sent unless you press Send.
  The developer uses feedback emails only to answer you and improve muna,
  and does not share them with anyone.
- **Device backups.** Like most apps, muna's data is included in your
  iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if you have those turned on. This
  is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by muna.

## What the developer receives

Nothing, unless you send a feedback email. muna has no way to see your
entries, balances, bills or settings.

## Deleting your data

- Delete an entry, goal, loan, reminder or attachment inside the app at any
  time.
- Deleting muna from your iPhone permanently deletes all of its data on the
  device. Backup files you exported yourself are not affected.
- To have a feedback email deleted, write to the contact address above.

## Children

muna is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any
information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same
address with a new effective date. A change that affects how your data is
handled will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
